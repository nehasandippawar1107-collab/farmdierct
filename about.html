Our Mission & Purpose

Connecting Indian Soil Directly to Your Table

FarmDirect was founded on a simple yet radical idea: farmers who feed our nation deserve 100% fair compensation, and consumers deserve food picked fresh rather than aged in middlemen warehouses.

The Traditional Agricultural Problem

Middlemen Take Up to 55% of Crop Value

In traditional wholesale supply chains, agricultural produce changes hands 5 to 7 times—through village aggregators, APMC mandi commission agents, regional wholesalers, transport cartels, and neighborhood retailers.

By the time vegetables and fruits reach households, they are days old, nutrient-depleted, and artificially ripened. Worse, the farmer often receives barely 20% to 35% of the final retail price.
