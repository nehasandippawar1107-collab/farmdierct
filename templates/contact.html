Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about direct farm-to-door delivery.

How fresh is the produce when delivered?

Unlike supermarkets where items spend 4-7 days in transit and warehouses, FarmDirect products are harvested by the farmer immediately upon receiving your order and dispatched via cold-chain routes.

How do farmers receive their money?

100% of the produce cost goes directly to the farmer without middleman commission cuts. Digital payments are credited immediately or upon successful delivery for COD orders.

What payment options are supported?

We support Cash on Delivery (Pay on Delivery), instant UPI (QR code scan or VPA), Credit/Debit Cards, and Net Banking across all major Indian banks.

How do you verify partner farmers?

Every farmer undergoes physical site verification by our agricultural field agents. Soil tests and organic certification records are inspected and maintained on the platform.
